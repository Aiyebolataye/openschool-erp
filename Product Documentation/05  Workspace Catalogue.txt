Enhancement Recommendation: Regional Structure

To make the platform suitable for very large education groups and future government deployments, I recommend adding an optional Regional Layer beneath the organization.

Example:

Organization
│
├── Region (North)
│   ├── Institution A
│   ├── Institution B
│
├── Region (South)
│   ├── Institution C
│   ├── Institution D

This would allow organizations to assign Regional Directors or Regional Managers who oversee only institutions within their region, improving scalability without affecting smaller organizations that don't need the feature.

This is the most important workspace in the platform. Nearly every module is managed here, so we should treat it as the reference implementation that other workspaces inherit from.

One design decision before we begin:

I recommend renaming School Administrator to Institution Administrator throughout the platform.

Why?

Your platform is an Educational Institution ERP, not just a school management system. An Institution Administrator works equally well for a secondary school, college, tutorial center, or future university support. Schools can still rename the role locally (e.g., "School Administrator" or "Registrar") through the Custom Roles feature.
