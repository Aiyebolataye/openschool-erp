04 — Core Platform Capabilities
1. Overview

The platform is an Educational Institution ERP Platform designed to support a wide variety of educational institutions without hardcoded assumptions about their organizational, academic, financial, or operational structures.

Every institution should be able to configure its own environment to match how it operates.

Core Principle

Nothing that varies between educational institutions should be hardcoded.

Institution-specific structures—including academic levels, grading systems, departments, fee structures, approval workflows, organizational hierarchies, report cards, and learning programs—must be configurable wherever practical.

Summer School & Credit Recovery

A Summer School Program is a specialized, typically paid program that institutions use for credit recovery.

Students who fail a course are automatically or manually flagged for credit recovery.

A flagged student may be enrolled in a Summer School (remediation) group to recover the failed course credit(s).

Enrollment may require payment depending on institutional policy.

Credit Recovery workflow:

1. Results identify a student who failed one or more courses.
2. The student is flagged for credit recovery.
3. Institution Admin (or a delegated staff member) enrolls the student in a Summer School remediation group.
4. A fee plan is attached (one-time, installments, or recurring), generating an invoice.
5. The student attends the remediation group and completes the course.
6. Upon completion, the failed grade is updated / credit is recovered.

Summer School remediation groups support the paid-program capabilities:

One-time payments
Recurring payments
Installments
Capacity limits
Waiting lists
Enrollment approval
Automatic invoice generation
Integration with the Finance Module
Attendance tracking
Course/grad recovery records

Rollover handling / failed-grade reuse is configurable per institution.
