02 — Platform Architecture
1. Overview

The Educational Institution ERP Platform is a multi-tenant, configurable SaaS platform designed to support one or many educational institutions under a single platform.

The architecture separates platform management from institution management while allowing institutions to independently configure their academic, administrative, financial, and operational structures.

## Entity Lifecycles

Major entities progress through defined lifecycle stages.

Student

Applicant → Admitted → Enrolled → Active → Graduated/Transferred → Archived

Staff

Applicant → Employed → Active → On Leave (Optional) → Resigned/Retired → Archived

Institution records are retained for historical reporting and auditing even after archival.

22. Platform Principles
Multi-tenant by design.
Configuration over customization.
Role-based access control.
Modular architecture.
Shared platform services.
Event-driven automation.
Consistent user experience across web and mobile.
Institution data isolation.
Scalable from a single institution to large organizations.
Ready for future expansion, including AI, CIAM, RFID, biometrics, and smart campus integrations.
Before moving on

This is one enhancement I strongly recommend adding because it will simplify both implementation and future maintenance:

Workspace Architecture

Instead of jumping directly into role workspaces, define a standard workspace structure that every role follows.

For example:

Workspace
│
├── Dashboard
├── Modules
├── Quick Actions
├── Notifications
├── Tasks
├── Reports
├── Calendar
├── Profile
└── Settings (if permitted)

Every role—School Admin, Principal, Teacher, Finance, Clinic, Reception, Parent, Student, etc.—would inherit this structure and only differ in the modules, permissions, and available actions.
